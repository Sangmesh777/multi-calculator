What's inside

Six focused tools rather than one crowded screen. Each one opens on its own tab.

01

Scientific calculator

Type an expression and it evaluates as you go.

  • Powers, roots, factorials, percentages
  • Trig in degrees or radians
  • History you can click back into
02

Health

Body metrics from the standard published formulas.

  • BMI with WHO categories
  • BMR and daily energy needs
  • Metric or imperial input
03

Finance

The three calculations people actually reach for.

  • Loan EMI with the interest breakdown
  • GST added or removed
  • SIP maturity and gain
04

Units

Eight categories, including the awkward ones.

  • Acres, hectares, square feet
  • US vs UK gallons, cups, tbsp, tsp
  • Bytes through terabytes, knots, stone
05

Currency

Live rates, with a fallback so it never breaks.

  • 42 currencies, Indian rupee by default
  • Dated snapshot when you're offline
  • Cash counter for notes and coins
06

Dates

Date maths that doesn't drift with time zones.

  • Days between two dates
  • Add or subtract days
  • No daylight-saving surprises

Why it's built this way

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no email, no server holding your numbers. Your theme and calculator history live in your own browser storage.

The maths stays on your device

Every calculation runs locally. The only network request in the whole app is fetching currency rates.

No framework, no dependencies

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Nothing to update, nothing to break, and you can read the whole thing in an afternoon.

Multi-Calculator

Health figures are population estimates, not medical advice. SIP projections assume a constant return. Currency rates come from a public source and fall back to a dated snapshot when offline.
